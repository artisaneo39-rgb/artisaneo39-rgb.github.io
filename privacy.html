Glisse: privacy policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

1. Data controller

The publisher of the Glisse app: Antoine Birabent, artisaneo39@gmail.com. Individual publisher.

2. What Glisse does not collect

Glisse has no account, no server, and collects no personal data for its own purposes. Your progress, settings, streak and statistics are stored on your device only and are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads in the Journey (levels) mode only, not in the daily challenge. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which acts as a controller for its own purposes. The SDK may process: advertising identifier or device-related identifiers, IP address (approximate location), advertising and ad-interaction data, crash logs and performance data. Purposes: ad delivery and measurement, analytics, fraud prevention. More information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): a consent form (Google UMP) is shown on first launch; no ad is requested before your choice. You can change it at any time in Settings > Ad privacy.
  • iOS (App Tracking Transparency): iOS asks your permission to use the advertising identifier. Declining does not change the game; ads are then non-personalised. Change it in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Legal basis: your consent (GDPR art. 6(1)(a)).

4. Glisse+ purchase

The one-time "Glisse+" purchase is processed by Apple (App Store). We receive neither your payment details nor your identity. The app checks your entitlement locally through StoreKit. Glisse+ removes ads.

5. Notifications

If you enable them, reminders are scheduled locally on your device, with no notification server.

6. Sharing

The share button uses the iOS share sheet; shared content is chosen by you and is not kept by the app.

7. Retention and transfers

Local data stays on your device until uninstall. Data processed by Google may be transferred outside the European Union under the safeguards described by Google.

8. Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability, and the right to withdraw consent at any time. For data processed by Google, exercise them with Google (myadcenter.google.com). Questions: artisaneo39@gmail.com. You may lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France: CNIL).

9. Children

Glisse is not specifically directed at children under 13 and asks them for no data.

10. Changes

This policy may change; the update date is shown at the top.
